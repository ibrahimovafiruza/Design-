Bu müqayisənin məqsədi eyni promptdan istifadə etməklə Claude Design, v0 və Stitch alətlərinin necə nəticə yaratdığını analiz etmək idi.
Müqayisə zamanı alətlərin promptu anlama səviyyəsi, dizayn yanaşması və UI keyfiyyətini dəyərləndirməyə çalışdım.

Website personal portfolio üçün nəzərdə tutulmuşdu. 

Istifadə etdiyim promt:

*"Design a premium UX/UI designer portfolio for Firuza Ibrahimova that blends modern editorial minimalism with mid-century modern aesthetics. Create a distinctive, serious, and timeless personal brand that feels curated rather than trendy.*

*Use a typography first approach with a strict grid system, generous whitespace, bold editorial headlines, and clean layouts. Introduce a refined mid-century color palette warm cream, deep forest green, burnt orange, mustard, muted teal, walnut brown, and charcoal—with flat colors only (no gradients).*

*Use the primary brand color as the background for selected sections to create rhythm and contrast throughout the page. Keep imagery minimal on the landing page instead of large photos, rely on typography, geometric compositions, subtle illustrations, abstract shapes, and thoughtful visual details to create impact. Incorporate paper-like textures, vintage-inspired graphic accents, and mid-century geometric patterns while keeping the UI contemporary and functional.*

*Showcase UX case studies with large, immersive product mockups, clear storytelling, and a structured design process. Use elegant micro-interactions, thin borders, soft rounded corners, and restrained animations that feel intentional and refined. Every section should feel like a beautifully designed editorial magazine, balancing bold color with generous whitespace. Avoid stock photography, glassmorphism, gradients, neon colors, excessive animations, and generic SaaS layouts. The overall experience should feel intelligent, confident, warm, museum-quality, and highly memorable—a portfolio that reflects a senior UX/UI designer with exceptional craftsmanship and a unique creative identity"*
